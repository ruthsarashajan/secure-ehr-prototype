## Supervisor Meeting - 18 June 2026

**Supervisor:** Raman Singh  
**Student:** Ruth Sara Shajan  
**Project:** Secure web-based access control and tamper-evident audit logging framework for simulated EHRs  
**Meeting time:** Morning

### Purpose of meeting

To discuss the early project direction after the project specification and confirm what should be prioritised before the interim report.

### Progress discussed

At this stage, the project specification had been submitted. The project idea was to build a simulated EHR web application using fake patient data, with a focus on access control and tamper-evident audit logging.

The planned prototype was expected to use:

- Python;
- Flask;
- SQLite;
- fake patient records;
- role-based access control;
- audit logging;
- hash-chain tamper detection.

### Supervisor feedback

The supervisor advised that the immediate focus should be on the written research foundation before rushing into implementation.

The main areas to focus on before the interim report were:

- introduction;
- literature review;
- research design;
- methodology;
- clear project scope;
- identifying the research gap;
- explaining what the project aims to achieve and how it will be evaluated.

The supervisor advised that the literature review should be completed before fully finalising the project scope.

The supervisor also recommended including diagrams to explain the planned system, such as:

- system architecture diagram;
- database design diagram;
- access-control flow diagram;
- audit-log/hash-chain design diagram;
- project timeline or Gantt chart.

The supervisor said implementation could be included if completed, but the interim report should mainly focus on literature, research design, and methodology.

### Decisions after meeting

The project would remain focused on a small simulated EHR prototype, not a full hospital or NHS system.

The interim report would focus mainly on:

- literature review;
- methodology;
- project scope;
- system design;
- planned evaluation;
- ethical/data considerations.

Implementation would come after the interim report stage.

### Action points

- Continue the literature review.
- Use the literature review to refine the final project scope.
- Prepare diagrams for the interim report.
- Keep the prototype focused on access control and tamper-evident audit logging.
- Avoid making the project too broad or turning it into a full healthcare management system.
- Complete and submit the interim report.

### Outcome

The advice from this meeting guided the interim report structure. The interim report was later completed and submitted around 2 July 2026.
